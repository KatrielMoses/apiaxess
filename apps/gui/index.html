    Updates

    The check is a plain GET of a static file on apiaxess.dev, once at startup and once a day. It sends only the app's version and platform (to pick the right download) — no identifiers, nothing about you or your sessions. Turn it off with "Check for updates" below or APIAXESS_UPDATE_CHECK=0. Downloads are checked against their published SHA-256 before anything is installed.
